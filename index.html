Aggregation Flow

How the platform works

  1. Connect APIs, feeds, or scraping workers for LinkedIn, Naukri, Wellfound, Instahyre, and company career pages.
  2. Normalize every posting into one schema: title, company, skills, source, posted date, work mode, and apply link.
  3. Rank listings by freshness plus preference-fit scoring based on selected role, skills, and experience band.
  4. Display everything in one dashboard so candidates can filter fast and apply without hopping platforms.
